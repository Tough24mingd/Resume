About Me

I'm a mechanical engineering student at the University of Massachusetts Amherst. My work spans CAD design, prototyping, and manufacturing.

My experience spans a few areas:

  • Design & prototyping: CAD modeling in SolidWorks and OnShape, plus rapid prototyping with 3D printing.
  • Manufacturing & simulation: Injection molding flow analysis in Moldex3D and structural analysis
  • Robotics & controls: Designing and building a custom multi-joint robotic arm, from mechanics to control software.
  • Computation: MATLAB and Python for engineering analysis.
