About Me

Cycloidal Drive

2026

Designing a custom cycloidal drive with a 20:1 reduction ratio, aimed at compact, high-torque, low-backlash motion for robotics.

  • Ring gear rollers made from ball bearings (13mm OD / 6mm ID).
  • Used the howtomechatronics.com DIY cycloidal drive design as a reference, scaling up the pin count for the application.
  • Output holes sized for 13mm bearings with a 2×1.5mm eccentricity offset.
